Privacy Policy for Attendance Plus

Application: Attendance Plus (Package: com.devkumar.attendanceplus)
Developer: Dev Kumar
Effective Date: September 16, 2026
Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: Attendance Plus is designed as an offline-first privacy-focused utility. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or attendance records to any third party. Your work attendance and overtime logs remain stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Store

When you use Attendance Plus, the following data is processed:

  • Work & Attendance Records: Daily statuses (Present/Work, Half Duty, Vacation, Sick leave, Emergency, Holiday), overtime hours, and optional text notes.
  • Calculation Settings: Daily wages, overtime hourly rates, currency preferences, and worker/employee display name.

All this data is stored locally in your device's browser storage or offline local app database. It is never transmitted to any private server without your explicit action (such as manually downloading a JSON backup file).

2. Advertising & Google AdMob

AdMob Integration Notice: This application displays third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to support free development and updates.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous data for advertising purposes, such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android devices.
  • Device identifiers, IP address, device model, operating system version, and general network connection data.
  • Ad performance interaction metrics (such as impressions and clicks).

Google uses this information to deliver personalized or contextual non-personalized ads according to Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google handles ad data and manage your ad preferences here:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy & Terms Policy
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

3. Permissions Required

Attendance Plus requires minimal permissions:

  • Network Access (Internet): Required solely to fetch advertisements from Google AdMob servers and check online connectivity. The core calendar, wage calculations, and reporting functions work 100% offline without internet.
  • Storage Access: Used to save local attendance records on your device and allow you to export or import your backup JSON files upon your request.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Attendance Plus does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The application is an employee/worker utility tool intended for general audiences and adult workforce management. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below so we can take immediate corrective measures.

5. Data Security and User Control

You have full ownership and control over your attendance records:

  • Data Deletion: You can wipe all stored records at any moment by using the in-app "Reset / Clear All" button in the header menu or by clearing app data in your device settings.
  • Data Portability: You can download a complete backup copy of your logs as a standard JSON file at any time from the Settings menu.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted directly on this URL with an updated "Effective Date".

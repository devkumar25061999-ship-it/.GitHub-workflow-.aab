# Amazon Appstore Publishing Guide for Attendance Plus

Publishing on the **Amazon Appstore** is one of the easiest and best decisions for your app.
Unlike Google Play Console, **Amazon Developer Account is 100% FREE** (no $25 fee, no 14-day 20 testers requirement, and approval is usually granted within 24 to 48 hours).

---

## 1. Files You Need for Amazon Appstore

All necessary files are prepared and generated by your repository:

| Requirement | File / Value in Your Project |
| :--- | :--- |
| **App Binary File** | **`AttendancePlus-Amazon-Release-APK`** (`.apk` generated via GitHub Actions) |
| **App Title** | `Attendance Plus` |
| **Package Name** | `com.devkumar.attendanceplus` |
| **App Icon** | `public/pwa-512x512.png` (512x512 PNG) |
| **Privacy Policy URL** | Your GitHub Pages URL (e.g. `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`) |
| **Category** | Productivity / Utilities / Business |

---

## 2. How to Download the Amazon APK File

1. Push your updated code to GitHub (or export to GitHub from the top-right menu).
2. Go to the **Actions** tab in your GitHub repository.
3. Click on **"Build Clean Android AAB (Google Play Bundle)"**.
4. Click **Run workflow**.
5. After 3-4 minutes, the workflow finishes and produces TWO files:
   - **`AttendancePlus-PlayStore-Release-AAB`** (for Google Play Store)
   - **`AttendancePlus-Amazon-Release-APK`** (for Amazon Appstore & Direct Android Installation)
6. Download **`AttendancePlus-Amazon-Release-APK`** to your phone or computer. Unzip it to get the `.apk` file.

## 3. Step-by-Step Guide to Publish on Amazon Developer Console

### Step 1: Create a Free Amazon Developer Account
1. Visit the [Amazon Developer Console](https://developer.amazon.com/).
2. Sign in with your Amazon account (or create a free new one).
3. Complete the free developer registration (select **Individual / Sole Proprietor**).

### Step 2: Add a New App
1. Go to **Dashboard** $\rightarrow$ **Amazon Appstore** $\rightarrow$ **Add New App**.
2. Select **Android**.
3. Fill in basic information:
   - **App Title:** `Attendance Plus`
   - **App SKU:** `com.devkumar.attendanceplus` (or `attendance-plus-01`)
   - **App Category:** `Productivity`
   - **Customer Support Email:** `devkumar25061999@gmail.com`
4. Click **Save**.

### Step 3: App Information & Media
1. **Short Description:**
   > Simple & fast daily work attendance register, overtime tracker, duty calendar, and monthly salary calculator.
2. **Long Description:**
   > Attendance Plus is an offline-friendly attendance and overtime recording tool designed for employees, factory staff, drivers, security personnel, and daily wage workers.
   > 
   > Key Features:
   > - 1-Tap Daily Attendance: Work, Half Duty, Vacation, Sick Leave, Emergency, and Holiday.
   > - Accurate Overtime (OT) Tracker: Record overtime in exact hours and tenths (0.5h, 1h, 2h).
   > - Automated Salary Estimation: Computes base pay from effective work days plus overtime earnings.
   > - Beautiful Clean Monthly Calendar: Continuous view with no blank days.
   > - Monthly Reports & Exports: One-click copy for WhatsApp or download clean CSV spreadsheet.
   > - 100% Offline & Private: No external database required. Your records stay on your device.
3. **Product Feature Bullets:**
   - One-tap marking for Full Duty and Half Duty
   - Overtime tracking with exact decimal precision
   - Automatic monthly wage and overtime salary calculations
   - Clean calendar layout and offline data persistence
   - Export monthly report to WhatsApp or CSV
4. **Icons & Screenshots:**
   - Upload `pwa-512x512.png` as the 512x512 app icon.
   - Upload 3 to 5 screenshots of your app (take screenshots from your phone or browser).

### Step 4: Content Rating & Privacy Policy
1. Answer the questionnaire (Mark as suitable for general audience / all ages).
2. Paste your Privacy Policy URL:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`
   *(Or the `/privacy.html` link).*

### Step 5: Upload APK & Submit
1. In the **APK Files** tab, upload your signed **`.apk`** file downloaded from GitHub Actions.
2. Amazon will perform an automatic pre-validation check (takes about 1-2 minutes).
3. Select target devices (Phone, Tablet, Fire Tablets).
4. Review your submission and click **Submit App**.

---

## 4. Approval Timeline
Amazon Appstore usually reviews and approves utility apps in **24 to 48 hours**. Once approved, it becomes live across millions of Android phones and Amazon Fire devices worldwide!
